## B. Proposed text

Brazil's Artificial Intelligence Plan for 2024–2028 identifies high-performance computing infrastructure and technical capacity as priorities for research and innovation (MCTI, 2025). The National Laboratory for Scientific Computing is developing a separate AI-oriented supercomputing installation in Macaíba, Rio Grande do Norte (LNCC, 2026). Agricultural research has related infrastructure needs. Embrapa's AgroDigital initiative has acquired high-performance computing and GPU servers to support research that requires substantial data processing and storage (Embrapa Agricultura Digital, 2024). These initiatives illustrate the importance of computing infrastructure across research domains.

Grain-storage planning raises a specific computational problem. The selection of warehouse sites and the sizing of storage and handling capacity combine discrete investment choices with flows that vary across products, periods and scenarios. A larger network increases the number of decisions and can place substantial demands on model construction, memory and solution time. This study uses experiments at NPAD/UFRN to examine the conditions under which deterministic and two-stage stochastic mixed-integer linear programs yield independently checked, interpretable plans for soybean and corn storage networks. The analysis relates measured computational requirements and attainable solution quality to the investment and operating decisions that the models return.
