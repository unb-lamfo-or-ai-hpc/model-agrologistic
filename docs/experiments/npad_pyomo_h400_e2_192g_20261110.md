# h400 E2 intermediate thread levels

E2 completes the first-repetition h400 W STO grid. It uses the same common
Pyomo model, frozen inputs, ordered matrix and bounds, hardware class and
runtime fingerprint as E1. The E1 observations at one and sixteen threads
remain under their original claims and source.

Every solve requests 192 GiB and 24 logical CPUs on an exclusive node,
with a verified 16-logical-CPU step. The native limit is eight hours, the
Slurm wall limit is 12 hours, the seed is 42 and the target gap is 1%.
Keep Gurobi `ConcurrentMIP=1` and HiGHS `parallel=on`. The E1 worker
threshold of 163.20 GiB, job-cgroup threshold of 172.80 GiB and cgroup v1
counter checks also apply. The fresh E2 campaign checks a 40 GiB storage
budget with the inherited 8 GiB outside reserve, and rechecks free space
and quota during execution. These are storage admission limits, separate
from the solver memory limits.

## Automated queue

The private campaign and queue have independent E2 paths
`.mvp2-pyomo-h400-e2-native8h-v1` and `.mvp2-pyomo-h400-e2-queue-v1`.
The E2 controller's explicit human invocation creates six solve jobs and
six collection jobs in hold. It verifies job identities, resources, batch
files and dependencies before releasing the whole graph. Each
`afterany` collector preserves its solver receipt and validates the prefix.
Each successor solve requires `afterok` on its predecessor gate and
replays the predecessor evidence before model construction. One solver
job runs at a time. The last gate reviews all six cells and releases no
further campaign.

A validated time limit without an incumbent remains a censored result
eligible for operational progression. A rejected gate blocks the next
solve. Preserve ambiguous submissions, failed attempts and the queue's
release receipts for inspection. Read the queue with the E2 controller's
`status` action, and export tables with the qualified campaign driver's
`tables` action. The new draft prepares these steps for qualification and
review, and does not submit either public or private jobs.
