Research dataset supporting reproducible deterministic and two-stage stochastic planning of Brazilian grain-storage networks. The planning framework combines warehouse investment and logistics decisions with service-first optimization, road-based network construction and independent solution validation.

Version 0.2.0 contains selected input workbooks, warehouse-population records, OSRM road-distance audits, reference evidence and preserved run artifacts. The comparative records cover thirteen nine-scenario optimization attempts at 215, 300 and 400 warehouses: seven with Gurobi and six with SCIP. Stage-level objectives, bounds, attained gaps, timings and memory observations support analysis under a nominal eight-hour optimization budget and a 10% optimality-gap criterion. Four Gurobi attempts completed the three-priority hierarchy with independently validated solutions.

The archives are accompanied by inventories, provenance records and checksums. The compact comparative-evidence archive is complemented by separate input, run-artifact, reference-evidence, OSRM-audit and inventory-report packages. File-level notices distinguish project-authored material from third-party data and their applicable terms.
